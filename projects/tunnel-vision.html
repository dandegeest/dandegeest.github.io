TunnelVision

AI Filmmaking System

TunnelVision is an AI filmmaking system for directing continuous first-person journeys through imagined worlds. It is not a prompt wrapper around a video model. The filmmaker specifies a journey; a Director structures the destinations; a Cinematographer figures out how to physically shoot each traversal; Camotion prepares motion-conditioned endpoint frames; and a video model films the camera travel between canonical viewpoints.

The concept originated with filmmaker Terran Boylan, who used the original TunnelVision technique on his YouTube channel to create long continuous POV simulations—selecting successive stills so a first-person camera appears to move through a coherent environment. That technique was later adapted for the Peter Gabriel Digging in the Dirt 50:50.dev project, where we used the playful name TunnelTV. This research asks what happens if that process becomes self-directing.

Preserve the world's rules while changing the viewer's question.

The current product workspace is Plan | Shoot. There is no edit suite. If the Director produces a sequence of canonical destinations and the Cinematographer films those legs with shared anchors and continuous locomotion, the finished journey is already the edit. The working loop is SPECIFY → DIRECT → CONSTRUCT → SHOOT. Research Phase 1 is complete; product development is underway, with assembled journeys through greenhouse interiors, Gravity City, forest tunnels, and the Wardrobe Loop.

This work is groundwork for an agentic filmmaking project at the Runway Hackathon.
